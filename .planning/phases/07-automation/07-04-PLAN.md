<objective>
Create `Existing Dashboards/fixlet_role_manager.ojo` (D-14, "Dereso Role Manager") as a working
IE9 OJO shell with its configuration block, its shared escaping helpers, and every **pure**
builder the generator needs — the "exactly one of N logged on" relevance builder (D-09/D-10),
the `<ComputerGroup>` BES-XML builder (Pattern 1), the per-role Task builder and the policy
`SingleAction` builder (Pattern 3) — together with the Node unit suite that pins their exact
output strings.

Purpose: this is the interface-first task. Plans 07-06, 07-07 and 07-08 wire UI and REST calls
around these functions; writing and testing them first means those plans never have to guess at
a contract. It also front-loads the single highest-impact bug this generator could ship: an
empty username list producing an unconstrained relevance that applies a role's policy action
fleet-wide (RESEARCH § Security Domain, Elevation of Privilege row).

**Plan-time decision recorded here (resolves RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 / Pitfall 7):** the
per-role policy action uses **create-new-then-stop-previous** on every Publish, not create-once.
This matches D-13's literal "policy-action redeploy" wording, keeps exactly one Open action per
role (the previous one is stopped by title match), correctly re-bakes `__ASSIGNED_BY__` when the
publishing operator changes, and gives a clean per-Publish audit trail that lines up 1:1 with
`RolePublishRun.RunID`. The ordering is deliberately **POST the new action first, then stop the
old one** so a mid-sequence failure leaves two idempotent actions running rather than zero
(Pitfall 4: least disruptive failure mode wins). The action-XML builder in this plan is shaped
for that flow; the stop call itself is implemented in plan 07-07.

Output: a loadable dashboard skeleton plus a green unit suite.
</objective>
